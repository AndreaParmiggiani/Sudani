What changed?

The course now follows grammar constructions rather than isolated vocabulary topics. Exercises from all original themes are mixed into paths such as verbal aspect, direction/prepositions, relative clauses, object pronouns, negation, and conditionals.

Example: بَمْشِي لِلسُّوق = “I go to the market”, whereas بِمْشِي عَلَى رِجْلِي = “I walk on foot”. The weak-construct tracker can revisit this distinction repeatedly.
